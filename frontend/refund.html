Refund Policy

Our commitment to your satisfaction

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

At Trend Mart Online, we are committed to ensuring your complete satisfaction with every purchase. If you are not entirely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions for returns, refunds, and exchanges.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products purchased from Trend Mart Online. If for any reason you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of receipt for a full refund or exchange.

Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, please ensure that:

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Items must be in original condition - unused, unwashed, and with all tags attached.
  • Items must be in the original packaging with all accessories and documentation.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required.
  • Return shipping costs are covered by Trend Mart Online for defective or incorrect items.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items that have been used, washed, or damaged by the customer.
  • Items returned beyond 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Personalized or custom-made items.
  • Perishable goods (food, flowers, etc.).
  • Personal care items (cosmetics, skincare, etc.) that have been opened.
  • Digital products and downloadable content.

Refund Process

Follow these steps to initiate a return or refund:

1

Shipping Costs for Returns

  • Free returns for defective, damaged, or incorrect items.
  • Free returns for orders over $50 (customer's choice).
  • Return shipping cost is the customer's responsibility for non-defective returns on orders under $50.
  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds

  • For COD orders, refunds are processed via bank transfer or store credit.
  • Refunds are issued to your bank account within 7-10 business days.
  • You may also choose store credit for faster processing (2-3 business days).
  • Please ensure accurate bank details are provided for COD refunds.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or variant, please contact our support team. We will assist you with the exchange process. Exchange shipping is free for all orders.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us immediately at support@trendmart.com with your order number and photos of the item. We will arrange for a free replacement or full refund, including shipping costs.

How to Initiate a Return

To start a return, please follow these steps:

  • Email us at returns@trendmart.com with your order number.
  • Call our support team at +1 234 567 890.
  • Use our Live Chat feature on our website.
  • Include your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason for return.
